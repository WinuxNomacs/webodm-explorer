# Sharing Center

**Explorer > Sharing Center**, in WebODM's main menu, manages every [anyone-with-the-link](/guide/sharing#anyone-with-the-link)
link of the projects you can change, in one place. WebODM's admins see every project's links. A project's own
**Share** panel stays for its links alone.

Each row shows the link's project (choose it to open the project's page with its Share panel), its label and
address, **Require Sign-In**, and under **Files** **Allow Downloads** and **Allow Source Photos** (to turn on or off, as
in the Share panel), when it was made
and by whom, and when it expires, marked **Expires Soon** within 7 days. On a phone the rows become cards.

## Finding a link

- **Search Labels, Projects and Users** matches a link's label, its project's name and who made it.
- **Active** (the default), **Expiring Soon**, **Expired** or **All Links**; each shows how many links it holds.
- A project, **Anyone with the Link** or **Require Sign-In**, and **Allow Downloads** or **View Only** narrow the
  list further.

The page's address keeps the search and filters, so a view can be bookmarked. A project's Share panel opens the
Sharing Center on that project's links with **All Links in the Sharing Center**.

## Creating a link

**Create Link**, at the top of the page: choose one of the projects you can change, then a label, an expiry,
**Allow Downloads**, **Allow Source Photos** and **Require Sign-In**, as in [the Share panel](/guide/sharing#anyone-with-the-link). A project
whose links Explorer Settings don't allow is listed but can't be chosen; for one whose links must all ask for a
login, **Require Sign-In** is ticked and can't be unticked. The new link is shown with its address selected, ready to
copy.

## Each link

- **Open**, **Embed** and **Copy**, as in the Share panel.
- **Edit**: the label, and the expiry (**Keep the Current Expiry**, or 7, 30 or 90 days from today, or **Never
  expires**). The address stays the same.
- **Revoke**, which stops the link at once, for everyone using it.

Where an admin's **Links to Private Projects** ([Administration](/guide/administration)) stops a project's links,
its rows say so.

## Expired links

An expired link opens nothing, but stays listed under **Expired** for 90 days after it expired, with:

- **Renew**: a new expiry from today (7, 30 or 90 days, or never). The same address works again, so a link already
  sent to someone doesn't need sending again.
- **Delete**, which removes it for good.

After 90 days an expired link is removed the next time that project's links change.
