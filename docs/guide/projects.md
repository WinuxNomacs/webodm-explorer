# Explorer's Home

**Explorer**, in WebODM's main menu, opens a drop-down with **Projects** and **Sharing Center**
([Sharing Center](/guide/sharing-center)). **Projects** opens a page of the projects you can view: your own and those shared with you.
WebODM's admins see every project. Projects without outputs yet are listed too, marked **No outputs yet**.

## Finding a project

- **Search Projects** matches names, descriptions and tags.
- **All**, **Mine** or **Shared with Me** narrows the list by owner.
- **Sort** by **Recent Activity**, **Name** or **Created**.
- **Has 3D** keeps projects with a point cloud, textured model or Gaussian splats; **Shared by Link** keeps those
  with an active link.
- **Clear Filters** goes back to everything.

The page's address keeps the search, filters, sort and page (24 projects to a page), so a view can be bookmarked or
sent to a colleague.

**Grid**, **List** and **Map** switch between the views below. Explorer remembers the one you last chose: the home
opens in it next time (Grid at first).

## Cards

Each project's card shows its picture, description and tags, a count per output type, its last activity, and its
owner when it isn't you. For users who can share the project, it also shows the project's active links, marked
**Expires Soon** when one expires within 7 days. Choose a card to open [the project's page](/guide/project).

## List

**List** shows the projects like folders in a file browser: on the left, every matching project by name (search and
the filters apply; there is no sort) with a small picture and its number of tasks; on the right, the selected
project, the first one at first. Choose another project, or move with the Up and Down keys, and the right side changes
without reloading the page; the address follows, so the browser's Back returns to the previous project.

The right side shows the project's name (with its active links), owner, task count, outputs and tags, and
description, with **Open Project Page**, **Share** (for users who can change it), **View as Visitor**, and in "..."
**Open in Dashboard** and **View All on Map**. Below them, a row for each task: its picture, name, date and facts,
and a button per view. Choose the picture or name to open [the task's page](/guide/project#a-task-s-page).

Up to 500 projects are listed; with more, search to find the others. On a phone the project list comes first;
choosing a project shows its side, with **Back to Projects**.

## Map

**Map** shows each project where its tasks were flown, as an outline around them with a marker that stays visible
at any zoom, over the basemap last chosen on Explorer's map pages (or WebODM's default one). Choose a project's
outline or marker for its name and task count, and a link to open it. Projects without located outputs are left
out.
